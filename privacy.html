Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

This policy covers two things: this website, and the personal LinkedIn publishing integration operated by Azhad Shahzad Shaik. Both are run by one individual. Neither is a commercial service and neither has customers.

This website

This site is a static portfolio. It does not have accounts, logins, comment forms, or a database. It uses Google Analytics to count page views in aggregate. That is the only third-party service embedded in the pages, and it is used to see which pages get read, not to identify individuals.

If you contact me by email, I keep the email. That is the whole of it.

The LinkedIn integration

A LinkedIn application is used to publish posts to a single LinkedIn account, my own, on a schedule. Its purpose is to replace manual posting with a scripted call.

  • It authenticates as one person, the account owner, using LinkedIn OAuth.
  • It is not offered to anyone else. There are no other users and no sign-up.
  • It writes posts. It does not read connections, messages, or any other member's data.
  • It stores an OAuth access token locally on the owner's machine so it can authenticate. The token is not transmitted anywhere except to LinkedIn's own API.
  • No data about any other LinkedIn member is collected, stored, processed, or shared.

What is not done

No data is sold. No data is shared with third parties. There is no advertising, no profiling, and no tracking of individuals across sites. Nothing is scraped from LinkedIn.

Retention

Analytics data follows Google Analytics' own retention settings. The OAuth token is held only while the integration is in use and is revocable at any time from LinkedIn account settings, which immediately ends the application's access.

Your choices

You can block analytics with any content blocker or by enabling Do Not Track. Nothing on this site requires it. If you would like an email you sent me deleted, ask and I will delete it.
